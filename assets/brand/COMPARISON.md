# Brand evolution

## Pack original (v1)
Half red / half yellow circle, white band, `BUS.SALE`, heavy outline. Strong flyer energy; reads retail / carnival more than marketplace trust.

## v2 Modern
Softer pill band, refined crimson / gold, separate “4” mark. Cleaner, still close to Pack DNA.

## v3 Atelier (current)
Full redesign for **modern · elegant · sophisticated**:

| Move | Why |
|------|-----|
| Claret + champagne + ivory | Premium warmth without signal-red / lemon noise |
| Double-ring seal | Auction-house / atelier calm; scales to 16px |
| Custom geometric **4** | Ownable monogram (shape memory) |
| `BUS · SALE` + wide tracking | Type-led confidence (Stripe / Brex lesson) |
| Georgia italic subtitle | Quiet craft against geometric sans |
| Ivory app ground | Soft canvas; sellers stay the artwork |

App theme colours and splash now follow the Atelier palette.
